WHY THREE DISCIPLINES

Most AI initiatives don't fail on the model. They fail on the system underneath it — or the strategy and leadership that never turned the plan into a running function.

We've spent our careers across all three: setting AI and data strategy at the leadership level, owning the P&L and team that deliver it, and — from the same technical grounding — architecting, reviewing, and securing the systems that have to carry it.

Hamingcs exists because those conversations are usually happening in different rooms, and shouldn't be.

OUR BACKGROUND

Two decades-plus of enterprise experience, earned on the way up — not assembled from a slide.

It started hands-on: building automotive diagnostic software in Java, then leading the architecture behind it — defining data models, distributing work across teams, and tracing production defects back to root cause when something didn't hold.

That technical grounding is what later underwrote the leadership: managing P&L on a project, then scaling into director and CTO-level ownership of an AI and data function — 300 people across the US, EU, Japan, China, and the Middle East, business and market strategy included, plus leading the cloudification and digitalisation of the technology stack underneath it. Hamingcs combines strategy, delivery, and security under one practice, in the order they were actually learned.

FAQ

Answers to the questions that come up most.

How engagements start, how we work, and what we protect.

What does Hamingcs actually do?
We pair strategy and hands-on leadership with AI/data expertise and the systems, security, and DevOps architecture needed to put it into production — any one discipline on its own, or all three as one engagement.
Who do you work with?
Mid-size to large organizations, primarily in automotive, banking, and technology, who need senior leadership on AI/data or enterprise systems without a full-time hire.
Do you work remotely?
Yes — Hamingcs is global and remote-first, with direct experience across European, Asian, and Middle Eastern operating environments. On-site work is available where an engagement calls for it.
How does an engagement start?
With a conversation about what you're building and where it's stuck. From there we scope whether it's advisory, interim leadership, or a defined project.
Can you cover strategy, AI, and systems architecture together?
Yes — that overlap is the reason Hamingcs exists. Engage us for any one discipline on its own, or all three under one point of contact.
Can you take P&L or leadership ownership, not just advise?
Yes — interim leadership engagements can include full P&L and team ownership at any scale, not just advisory input, drawn from direct experience running budgets from focused projects up to a large, multi-region P&L and a 300-person team across the US, EU, Japan, China, and the Middle East.
Do you work on retainer or fixed scope?
Both. Advisory is typically ongoing, interim leadership is embedded for a defined period, and project delivery is scoped and timelined up front.
Do you work under NDA?
Yes, as standard. Confidentiality and data-handling terms are agreed before any engagement begins.
Do you help with compliance frameworks?
Yes — including readiness assessments against SOC 2 and ISO 27001, and AI governance work as regulation such as the EU AI Act catches up with deployment.
Is security part of the AI work too?
It has to be. Threat modeling and release architecture are part of the first design conversation on every engagement, not a review bolted on at the end.
